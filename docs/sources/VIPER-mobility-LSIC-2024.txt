  VIPER Rover Overview

Mass
      · Launch: 490 kg (with lander I/F), roving: 447 kg

Dimensions: 1.7m x 1.7m x 2.5m

Power
      · Solar arrays: 320W per panel (450W on corner)
      · Battery capacity (start of life @ 0C): 5,420 W-hr

Communications
      · X-band Direct-To-Earth (no relay)
      · Transmit: 250 sps / 8 Msps (min/max)
      · Receive: 250 sps / 75 ksps (min/max)
      · Gimballed high gain + fixed low-gain antennas

Unique Environments
      · Temperature extremes
            · 50 hours survival in low power mode
            · 9 hours ops in permanently shadowed region
      · Demanding lighting
      · Uncertain lunar pole trafficability

Mobility Overview

· Four-wheel architecture
      · Slopes up to 15 deg (reqt)
      · Obstacles to 15 cm (reqt)
      · Mobility system qualified to 2x20 km surface life
      · Launch restraints on suspension and steering

· Wheel Modules
      · Wheels: diameter 50 cm, with 2.5 cm grousers, spoked
      · Drive: top speed 20cm/s, nominal speed 10 cm/s
      · Steering: explicit with +/-50 deg range
      · Suspension: adjustable with individual wheel downforce control
             · IMU feedback used for chassis pitch/roll control
             · Adjustable ground clearance from 7-43cm

· Motor Controllers
      · Each avionics box controls 6 BLDC actuators
             · 20A output current peak for 10 sec per axis
             · Two wheel modules controlled by each box
             · Current, voltage, temperature, load cell measurement
             · Cascaded control loops: Position -> Velocity -> Current
      · Contained within warm box
Mobility Cont.

· Driving Modes
      · Omni-directional driving with sun on corner
            · Maximizing power generation
      · Hybrid mobility
            · Entrapment escapement
            · Inch-worming/crawling
      · Precision movement
      · Slip/embedding detection

· Moon Gravity Representation Units (MGRU)
      · VIPER Flight Unit unable to support its own weight
         on earth
      · Basic configuration is lunar weight
            · Mobility only: power, control, computing off
               board
      · Can "violate" lunar weight to add capability
            · Examples: cameras, mast, lighting
      · V&V, driver training, ops scenario development

Closing Remarks

· VIPER approach
      · Purchase proven components when available
            · Example: same core battery components as Orion
      · Do engineering development when possible
            · Example: IAU/PDU derived from LADEE mission's IAU/PDU
      · Develop technology development only necessary

· Many on the team spent much of their career working tech development
      · Investment in people along with the technologies the people develop
