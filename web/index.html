      How to read this

      The two rovers

      Surveyor A

      Camera and neutron sensor, no drill. Finds sites, maps them, and flags dangerous ground.

      Prospector B

      Arm and drill. Reads what the Surveyor found from the chain, then drills to confirm the ice.

      On the ground

      • Amber ringA possible site, relayed by the satellite
      • Red patchIce found here
      • Red dashed ringA flagged hazard; rovers plan around it
      • Coloured lineThe route a rover is following
      • Red ringA crater with ground steeper than 15° inside it, which the route planner will not cross. Shallow craters are not ringed. On the overhead map, red marks that steep ground

      Lidar view

      Each rover carries a forward lidar beside its camera: 120° wide, 30 m range (Calvin’s sim/lidar.py). The Camera | Lidar switch shows what each scan told the rover, seen from above: forward is up, the rover is the white mark at the bottom, rings every 5 m.

      • GreyGround the lidar judged safe
      • AmberCrater floor; the planner avoids it
      • RedSteeper than 15°: no-go
      • BlackNo return, or too few to judge

      On chain

      Each record holds a result and a 32-byte fingerprint of the full report. The report itself stays off chain. Verify re-checks the fingerprint, which proves the report has not changed.
