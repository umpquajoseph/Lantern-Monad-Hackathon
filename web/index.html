LanternScout campaigns
Runs

Each run is one campaign: one world, drawn at random from its seed, and one set of design choices. One to five identical Scouts share a dock and fly sorties into a shadowed crater. They log ice finds with their position bounds, check each other's finds, and add newly seen craters to a shared map. Click a run to see its map grow sortie by sortie.

Position error is sampled from ShadowNav's published results (Cauligi et al. 2023, Table 2). Every setting carries its source or is marked assumed in sim/settings.py. Click a column to sort.
